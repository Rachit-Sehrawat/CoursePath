# CoursePath

## About the project

CoursePath is a Python project for college course planning and academic progress. It is designed to help students organize course and student information, understand course prerequisites, plan semesters, avoid timetable conflicts, and track degree progress.

## What it is for

Students can use CoursePath to keep course records organized and check whether they have completed the prerequisites for a course they want to take. The wider project is planned as five connected modules that share course and student information.

## Project modules

- **Course & Student Management** — maintain course details and student records.
- **Prerequisite & Eligibility** — store prerequisite requirements and check course eligibility.
- **Semester & Credit Planning** — plan courses by semester and monitor credit limits.
- **Timetable & Conflict Management** — organize class times and identify schedule conflicts.
- **Academic Progress & Reports** — track completed and remaining credits and summarize academic progress.

## Current implementation status

The current starter includes runnable course and student management, plus prerequisite and eligibility checks. Semester planning, timetable management, and academic progress reporting are planned for future development. By equal module count, 2 of 5 modules are implemented (40%); the percentage may differ under the instructor's grading rubric.

Course and student records are stored in CSV files in the `data` folder. The included sample records are fictional.
